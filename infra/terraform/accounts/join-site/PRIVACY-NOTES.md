# Candidate privacy policy gap

The current `edium.online/privacy/` text describes platform accounts, educational content, technical data, and educational-product purposes. It does not explicitly cover candidate applications, resumes, recruiting-purpose processing, candidate retention, or the internal reviewers.

Before publishing `/join/`, the owner or counsel should approve an update containing at least:

- legal name and contact details of the personal-data controller;
- the purpose of reviewing possible cooperation with Edium and contacting the applicant;
- all collected categories, including resume contents, contest source code, explanations, links, timestamps, test results, reviewer notes, 1–5 task scores and review status;
- applicable legal basis and exact consent wording;
- retention/deletion rules for rejected, inactive, accepted, and withdrawn applications;
- processor/recipient categories, including Yandex Cloud and the selected email provider;
- processing geography and any applicable cross-border-transfer language;
- the staff identities or role allowed to read applications;
- a real channel for access, correction, withdrawal, and deletion requests;
- withdrawal and backup-handling rules.

Contest capability tokens are not stored in plaintext or written to application logs. The runner receives only a task id and source code, has no browser identity or resume data, and returns a bounded test report. Reviewer scores are not an automated hiring decision and must not be presented as one.

The staged form names the candidate-review purpose and links to the policy, but this does not replace legal approval. No controller identity or legal details were invented.
